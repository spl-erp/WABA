# Teams, numbers and roles (one place to set them)

**The Team is the only place that says who works where.** Open Waba > Setup > Teams.

1. Each department is a team under the top-level team. Add its **numbers** (a number may be used by more than one department).
2. Add its **people**, each an **Agent** or a **Team Lead**, with their limits (max open chats, rep code, languages).
3. Press **Setup check**. It lists what is wrong (a person in two teams, a number nobody uses, no Team Lead) and what Apply will change.
4. A Waba Admin presses **Apply to numbers** once the check shows no errors. From then on:
   - each Number's agent list is a copy of its teams' people (Team Lead = Supervisor, Agent = Agent), so do not edit it on the Number;
   - the Waba Agent / Waba Supervisor role is given when someone joins a team and taken away when they are in no team.

Who sees what: a Waba Admin sees every chat. A Team Lead sees chats assigned to anyone in their team or below, everything on a number only their
branch uses, and, on a number shared with another department, only what is unassigned. An Agent sees only their own chats.
System Manager alone no longer shows chats.

A Team Lead can add and remove the people of their own branch and change their limits. Numbers, the tree, routing rules and sending
limits stay with Waba Admin.

**Return to waiting list** (chat menu, with a note) takes a chat off its person. Routing leaves it alone until a Team Lead hands it on with Transfer.

## Marketing templates one to one (for now)

Waba Settings > **Allow Marketing templates** is on. The inbox and the Lead / Contact / Customer dialogs then list approved Marketing
templates next to Utility ones. A Marketing send needs the same consent as a Utility one; a STOP, a STOP PROMO or a marketing "No"
blocks it. Marketing messages cost much more. Automatic notifications stay Utility only. Untick it to go back to Utility only.
